A stand mixer is a ten-year purchase, so the right call depends less on headline
specs and more on how often you bake and what you make.

## Quick take

If you bake weekly, the KitchenAid Artisan earns its price through longevity and
accessories. Bake occasionally? The Hamilton Beach does the same core job for far
less. The Cuisinart sits in between with the most raw power.
