The Ninja AF101 has been the internet's default air fryer recommendation for years.
We wanted to know whether that still holds in 2026, with newer, flashier models on
the shelf.

## Who it's for

If you cook for one or two people and mostly reheat, roast vegetables, or make
frozen foods crispy, this is all the air fryer you need — [see the current price
on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07FDJMC9Q). Families cooking large batches
should size up.
