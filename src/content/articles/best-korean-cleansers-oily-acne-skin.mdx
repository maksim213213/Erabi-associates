Oily and acne-prone skin doesn't need a harsher cleanser — it needs a *smarter* one.
The K-beauty approach is the **double cleanse**: an oil or balm first to dissolve
sunscreen and sebum, then a gentle water-based wash. These picks cover both steps plus
an exfoliating option, chosen on ingredients and aggregated reviews.

## How to double cleanse (the whole point)

1. **On dry skin**, massage in an **oil or balm** (Clean It Zero or the Anua oil) to melt
   off SPF, makeup and sebum. Add water to emulsify, then rinse.
2. **Follow with a gentle gel** (COSRX Low pH) to clean what's left.

Only the evening needs a double cleanse — in the morning, a single gel wash is plenty.

> **Not medical advice.** Introduce exfoliating cleansers slowly, and don't stack them
> with other strong actives at first. Patch-test before regular use.
