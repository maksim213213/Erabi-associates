Breakout-prone skin gets pushed toward harsh, drying products — which often makes things
worse by damaging the barrier. The K-beauty approach is the opposite: **one real active
(BHA), and everything else gentle and hydrating.** Here's a simple, barrier-friendly order.

## The golden rule: one active, introduced slowly

The only "strong" step here is the **BHA (Step 3)**. Start it **2-3 nights a week** and
build up as your skin tolerates it. Everything else is gentle enough for daily use. On
nights you use BHA, skip other exfoliants.

## AM vs PM

- **Morning:** Cleanser → Toner → Essence → **Sunscreen.**
- **Night:** Cleanser → Toner → **BHA (a few nights/week)** → Essence → spot patch as needed.

> **Important — not medical advice.** This is a cosmetic routine, not acne treatment.
> Persistent, painful, or cystic acne is a medical issue: see a dermatologist, especially
> before combining actives or if you're pregnant. Patch-test everything and stop anything
> that stings or worsens your skin.
