Sunscreen is the single highest-impact step in any routine — and it's exactly the step
Korean and Japanese formulas do better than almost anyone. These are cosmetically
elegant enough that you'll actually want to wear them every day, which is the whole
point. Picks below are grouped by skin type, based on ingredient profiles and aggregated
reviews.

## How we picked

We prioritized daily-wear feel (no white cast, no stinging, plays well under makeup),
broad-spectrum **SPF50/PA++++** protection, and strong review histories — not just hype.

> **Not medical advice.** SPF needs vary by person and climate; reapply every ~2 hours
> outdoors. Patch-test any new sunscreen before daily use.

## Quick decode: what "PA++++" means

SPF measures UVB (burning) protection; **PA++++** is the highest rating for UVA (aging)
protection. Korean and Japanese sunscreens almost always list both — one reason they're
so popular for anti-aging.
