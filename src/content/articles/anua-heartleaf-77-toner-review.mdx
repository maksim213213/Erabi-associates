Anua's Heartleaf 77 Soothing Toner has been the **#1 facial toner on Amazon** and a
non-stop TikTok favorite. We looked at what actually earns that spot — the formula, who
it's for, and its real limits — based on the ingredient list and thousands of aggregated
reviews.

## What it is (and isn't)

This is a **hydrating, soothing toner**: 77% heartleaf extract plus hyaluronic acid for a
thin layer of calm, lightweight moisture right after cleansing. It is **not** an
exfoliating toner (no AHA/BHA) — so it won't unclog pores or resurface texture. Buying it
for the wrong job is the main reason for the occasional disappointed review.

## Who should buy it

If your skin is **sensitive, red or dehydrated**, this is an easy, low-risk staple that
plays well under everything else. If your priority is **acne or texture**, pair it with a
dedicated active (or look at an exfoliating toner instead).

> **Not medical advice.** "Soothing" claims describe cosmetic feel, not treatment.
> Patch-test first if your skin reacts easily.
