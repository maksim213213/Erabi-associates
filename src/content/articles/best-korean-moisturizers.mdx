The moisturizer is the step that *seals in* everything you layered before it, so the
right one depends less on brand and more on how oily or dry your skin runs. Below,
one strong pick per skin type — chosen on ingredient profiles and aggregated reviews.

## Match the texture to your skin

- **Very dry / sensitive →** ceramide barrier cream (Illiyoon).
- **Normal / combination →** a light gel-cream (COSRX Snail 92).
- **Oily →** a water-gel that won't add shine (belif Aqua Bomb).
- **Dry or nighttime →** a richer nourishing cream (Dynasty Cream).

Apply to slightly damp skin — right after your essence or serum — to trap the most
hydration.

> **Not medical advice.** Patch-test new products and introduce one at a time so you
> can tell what's working.
