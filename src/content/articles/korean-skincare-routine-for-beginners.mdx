If you've seen "glass skin" all over Pinterest and TikTok and felt lost in a
10-step routine, start here. You don't need ten products — you need **five, in the
right order, used consistently.** This guide is built from the most-reviewed
K-beauty staples on Amazon, chosen for gentle formulas and beginner-friendly
textures rather than hype alone.

> **How we chose:** this is a research-based roundup — we compared ingredient
> lists, formulator notes and thousands of aggregated user reviews. It isn't
> medical advice. Everyone's skin differs, so patch-test new products on your
> inner arm for a day or two before putting them on your face.

## The one rule of layering: thin to thick

Apply in the order above — watery textures first, richest last — so each layer can
absorb. The only timing split to remember:

- **Morning (AM):** Cleanser → Toner → Essence → **Sunscreen.** Sunscreen is always
  the last morning step, and the one that actually protects against aging.
- **Night (PM):** Cleanser → Toner → Essence → **Serum.** The Glow Deep Serum goes
  here because alpha-arbutin works best away from daylight.

## Who this routine is for

Normal, combination and sensitive skin will all get along with these formulas —
they're low-irritation by design. If your main goal is hydration and an even, glowy
base, this is a complete starting point. You can add a dedicated moisturizer or a
weekly exfoliant later, but master these five first.
