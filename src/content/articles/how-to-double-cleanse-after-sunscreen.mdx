If you wear sunscreen, the evening cleanse matters. But “remove it thoroughly” does
not mean scrub hard or use three different washes. A double cleanse is simply a
two-part reset: one formula loosens oil-soluble layers such as SPF and makeup; the
next rinses away what is left.

For most people, this is an **evening-only** routine. In the morning, a splash of
water or one gentle cleanser is usually enough.

## When a double cleanse makes sense

Use it on evenings when you have worn water-resistant sunscreen, reapplied SPF,
worn makeup, or simply feel an oily film at the end of the day. It can also be useful
after a beach day or long commute.

You probably do **not** need it twice a day. If your skin is dry, irritated or feels
tight after cleansing, doing less — not adding a stronger cleanser — is the sensible
first adjustment.

## The two-minute method

1. **Start with dry hands and dry skin.** Massage one or two pumps of cleansing oil
   gently over your face, including around the hairline and jaw. Do not rush into
   adding water: this is the part where the oil loosens sunscreen and makeup.
2. **Emulsify, then rinse.** Wet your hands and keep massaging until the oil turns
   milky. Rinse with lukewarm water; very hot water is more likely to leave skin
   feeling uncomfortable.
3. **Use a gentle gel cleanser.** Massage briefly, rinse, then pat your face dry
   with a clean towel. Follow with your normal evening routine while skin is still
   slightly damp.

The finish you want is *comfortable and clean*. “Squeaky” is not a sign that the
routine worked better.

## Choose the first cleanser by what you wore

| At the end of the day | Best first step |
| --- | --- |
| Light daily sunscreen, no makeup | A light cleansing oil or balm |
| Water-resistant SPF or several reapplications | Oil or balm, then a gentle gel |
| Makeup plus sunscreen | Oil or balm, then a gentle gel |
| Skin already dry or reactive | Keep the oil massage short; use the mildest gel you own |

An oil and a balm do the same job here. Pick the texture you will actually use: oil
spreads quickly, while balm is easier for some people to travel with.

## Common ways people overdo it

- **Cleansing twice in the morning and again at night.** Save the two-step routine
  for the evening.
- **Using an exfoliating cleanser as the second step every day.** The removal step
  is already complete; acids and scrubs are optional treatment products, not a
  requirement.
- **Massaging for several minutes.** Gentle and brief is enough. More friction is
  not more effective.
- **Skipping moisturizer after a thorough cleanse.** If your skin needs it, follow
  with a simple moisturizer to restore comfort.
